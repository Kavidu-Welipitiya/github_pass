Major Objectives of the Job: 
Human Resources plays a pivotal role in developing and executing people strategies that actively support business growth, organizational excellence, and a thriving high-performance culture across People's Leasing & Finance PLC. By partnering closely with senior management, this position ensures that all strategic human resource initiatives are seamlessly aligned with the company’s broader corporate objectives and business strategy.

1.	Strategic HR & Organizational Effectiveness
I.	Partners with senior leadership to design and implement strategic HR initiatives that drive corporate goals forward. 
II.	Designs, develops, and implements robust Key Performance Indicator (KPI) frameworks and productivity measurement systems across all business units. 
III.	Applies workforce planning techniques, job evaluation methodologies, competency mapping, and manpower optimization strategies to ensure the organization operates efficiently. 
IV.	Utilizes HR analytics and data-driven decision-making to support strategic planning and boost overall organizational performance.
2.	Talent Acquisition & Performance Management 
I.	Introduces and actively manages modern selection techniques and recruitment methodologies, including competency-based interviews, behavioral assessments, and assessment centers. 
II.	Enhances the Company's Performance Management Framework using modern evaluation techniques, such as performance calibration, 360-degree feedback, and Performance Improvement Plans. 
III.	Develops comprehensive Competency Frameworks that cover leadership, managerial, functional, technical, and behavioral competencies across all employee categories.
3.	Employee Engagement & Talent Development 
I.	Leads engaging initiatives focused on talent management, succession planning, retention strategies, and leadership development to build a strong, future-ready talent pipeline. 
II.	Fosters a high-performance, customer-centric culture that truly promotes collaboration, accountability, employee wellbeing, and continuous learning. 
III.	Develops thoughtful behavioral assessment criteria to accurately evaluate leadership potential, emotional intelligence, resilience, and teamwork capabilities.







4.	Compliance, Governance & Policy Management
I.	Reviews, develops, and implements HR policies, procedures, and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) to ensure operational consistency. 
II.	Ensures all HR governance frameworks strictly comply with Sri Lankan Labour Laws, regulatory requirements, and corporate governance standards applicable to Licensed Finance Companies.
